## 🪷 What is Krishna AI?

**Krishna AI** is a conversational AI companion inspired by the wisdom,
philosophy, and teachings traditionally associated with Lord Krishna.

It is built around a simple idea:

> **Don't just give people answers. Help them see clearly.**

Instead of treating every message as a simple question-answer exchange,
Krishna AI is designed to understand the user's situation, recognize
the deeper conflict when appropriate, introduce relevant wisdom, gently
challenge flawed assumptions, and help the user find clarity.

It does **not** attempt to make every conversation spiritual.

A technical question should receive a technical answer.

A practical problem should receive practical guidance.

An emotional struggle may require empathy and reflection.

A philosophical question may deserve deeper exploration.

The conversation should adapt to the person and the situation.
